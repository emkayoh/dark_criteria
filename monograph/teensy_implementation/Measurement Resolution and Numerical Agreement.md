### Measurement resolution and numerical agreement

Agreement between the directed real-time VARPRO solution and the full global VARPRO solution was typically at approximately five decimal places or better. This is already comparable with, or finer than, the effective resolution of the underlying observations. Response time is recorded to 1 ms, corresponding to approximately \(1.7\times10^{-5}\) min. Stimulus intensity is represented by a 16-bit PWM value spanning approximately 48.16 dB, calculated as \(10\log_{10}(2^{16}-1)\), which corresponds to a nominal quantisation of approximately \(7.35\times10^{-4}\) dB per PWM step. Although fitted parameters may numerically agree to finer precision than the raw measurements themselves, differences beyond this scale are unlikely to be experimentally meaningful. The observed five-decimal-place agreement therefore provides a practically stringent demonstration that the directed real-time refinement reproduces the full global VARPRO solution.
